Welcome

A quiet room above an old harbour town

Mornings begin with light on the water and the sound of the town waking below. Heart of Batroun is a small guesthouse made for slow days: a glass terrace that holds the sea, cool tiled floors, soft linens, and evenings that drift from the stone lanes of the old souk to the last gold of sunset.

Stay a weekend or a fortnight. Everything you need is here, and the rest of Batroun is a short walk away.

Guest Words

Discover Batroun

One of the oldest towns on the Mediterranean

Phoenician sailors knew this shore. Today it is a town of stone churches, fishing boats and long, unhurried evenings.

  1. I

    The Old Town

    Limestone lanes, arched doorways and the old souk, with St. Stephen’s Cathedral facing the little fishing port.

  2. II

    The Phoenician Wall

    A natural sea wall shaped by ancient hands, where waves break against stone that has stood for millennia.

  3. III

    The Beaches

    Clear water, pebbled coves and sandy stretches along the coast, from quiet swims to lively beach clubs.

  4. IV

    The Table

    Grilled fish by the harbour, mezze under the vines, and a glass of Batroun’s famous fresh lemonade.

  5. V

    The Sunsets

    Every evening the sky turns copper over the water. Find a seat by the sea, or simply return to the terrace.

Location

Finding us

Batroun sits on the coast about 50 km north of Beirut, roughly an hour by car along the coastal highway, traffic permitting. From Beirut–Rafic Hariri International Airport, allow around 75 minutes.

Private parking is waiting on arrival. Your exact address and self check-in details are shared on WhatsApp once your stay is confirmed.
